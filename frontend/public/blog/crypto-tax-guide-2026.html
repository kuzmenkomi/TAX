Introduction to 2026 Crypto Taxes

The landscape of cryptocurrency taxation has undergone significant changes heading into 2026. Tax authorities worldwide, including the IRS and various LATAM agencies, have implemented stricter reporting requirements for digital assets.

Capital Gains vs. Income

Most crypto transactions are classified as either capital gains/losses or ordinary income. Selling Bitcoin for a profit? That is a capital gain. Earning staking rewards? That is usually treated as income at the fair market value on the day you received it.

Essential Reporting Requirements

In 2026, you must report all "disposals" of cryptocurrency. This includes selling for fiat, trading one coin for another, and even using crypto to buy goods or services.
